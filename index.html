Portfolio strategy

Every number, chart and table recalculates when you change a choice. All four strategies spend the same budget, equal to the cost to secure, so they compare on equal terms. Cash flow matching is a theoretical benchmark: one zero coupon bond per payment date, gap zero by construction. Bullet and barbell hold two Treasuries weighted so that the total DV01 gap is zero. Ladder spreads the budget equally over seven Treasuries.

The Portfolio holdings tile shows what each strategy would hold, not what anyone should buy.

Obligations DV01

Value change of the obligations if all rates rise by 0.01 percentage points (1 basis point). Negative because values fall when rates rise. The bar shows how the sensitivity splits by maturity bucket.

Portfolio holdings

What the selected strategy would hold if it were built today with a budget equal to the cost to secure the selected obligations. It shows what each strategy would hold, not what anyone should buy.

Instrument: the Treasury held (for cash flow matching, one zero coupon bond per payment date). Weight: share of the budget. Amount to invest: money spent on it, in USD millions (budget times weight). Face value: the amount repaid at maturity; notes and bonds are bought at par (100), so face value equals the amount, bills and zero coupons are bought at a discount, so face value is higher. Yield or coupon: the par yield, which is also the coupon of a par bond (zero rate for zero coupons). Duration: years; how much the value moves, in percent, for a 1 percentage point move in rates. DV01 contribution: value change of that holding, USD millions, if all rates rise by 1 basis point. Share of portfolio DV01: its part of the portfolio's total rate sensitivity.

The amounts add up to the cost to secure, the weights to 100% and the DV01 contributions to the portfolio DV01 shown in the gap tile; the page checks this at every recalculation.

All strategies side by side: amount to invest per Treasury, USD millions. The same budget, spread in different ways.

Illustrative construction for comparison. It is not a recommendation to buy or sell. Real trading would involve prices, lot sizes, costs and a view on rates that this page does not use.

Scenarios

Custom scenario: shock per tenor in basis points. It is applied to the selected strategy like the other scenarios. The 3 Mo shock does not change any result today: the obligations and every strategy start at 0.5 years. It would matter only if the obligations' duration fell below about half a year, when the bullet strategy would hold the 3 Mo bill.
